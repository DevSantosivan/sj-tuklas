01

Information we collect

Depending on how you use SJ Tuklas, we may collect information such as account details, business information, contact information, booking information, and information related to your use of the platform.

Information may be provided directly by you when you create an account, submit a business listing, make a booking, update your profile, or use other platform features.

We may also collect technical and usage information necessary for the operation, security, and improvement of the platform.

02

How we use information

Information may be used to provide platform functionality, manage accounts, display business listings, process available platform interactions, improve the platform, and communicate important service information.

We may also use information to maintain platform security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot technical issues, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

03

Customer accounts

If you create a customer account, SJ Tuklas may collect information necessary to create and manage your account, such as your name, email address, and other information you choose to provide.

Depending on the features you use, we may also process information associated with your activity on the platform, such as bookings, saved businesses, or other account-related interactions.

Customer account information may be used to provide account functionality, maintain activity history, support available bookings, and improve customer services provided through SJ Tuklas.

04

Business information

Business owners may submit information such as business names, descriptions, addresses or locations, contact information, operating hours, images, services, menus, and promotions.

Information intentionally submitted for a public business listing may be displayed to visitors and customers of SJ Tuklas.

Business owners are responsible for ensuring that information they submit is accurate and that they have the appropriate rights to publish submitted content.

05

Information sharing

We may share information when necessary to operate the platform, provide requested functionality, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or support legitimate service providers.

Information that is intentionally made public as part of a business listing may be visible to other visitors and users of SJ Tuklas.

We do not intend to make private account information publicly available unless disclosure is necessary for the requested functionality, authorized by the user, or required or permitted by applicable law.

06

Cookies and similar technologies

SJ Tuklas may use cookies, local storage, session technologies, or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, understand platform usage, and improve the user experience.

Some technologies may be necessary for authentication, security, and basic platform functionality.

07

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials and devices.

08

Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, support platform operations, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain legitimate business or security records.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

09

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, or other processing of your personal information.

You may also be able to update certain account information through the platform or contact SJ Tuklas regarding available privacy-related requests.

Requests may be subject to reasonable verification and any limitations or exceptions provided by applicable law.

10

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the platform, features, practices, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the applicable update date.

11

Contact

Questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy can be directed to the SJ Tuklas team.
